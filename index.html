00B Master Repository Governance

The governance standard for protecting, maintaining, modifying, distributing, recovering, and evolving the Darkmode master repository.

Core Rules

  • One authoritative source of truth.
  • The master repository is not a general collaboration environment.
  • Reference master objects rather than duplicating them.
  • Project discoveries do not become standards without review.
  • Playbooks are controlled operational distributions derived from approved master information.
  • Meaningful changes must be traceable, reviewable, versioned, and recoverable.

01B Company Profile

Operating Name: Darkmode

Website: darkmodeworks.com

Business Type: Creative, marketing, technology, and business systems company.

Darkmode combines human strategy and creative direction with technology, AI, specialized talent, and repeatable systems to deliver high-quality work without requiring the traditional overhead structure of a large agency.

Operating Model

A lean Core organization supported by the Darkmode Network and external Partners. Darkmode retains ownership of strategy, standards, systems, quality control, client relationships, and project direction.

01D Organizational Structure

Darkmode is designed as a lean core organization supported by a flexible network of specialists assembled according to the requirements of the work.

CORE

Leadership, strategy, client ownership, operational control, quality standards, and stewardship of the Darkmode Operating System.

DARKMODE NETWORK

A trusted community of specialized talent selected because their skills, experience, perspective, or capabilities make them the right people for the work.

PARTNERS

External businesses, vendors, platforms, consultants, and specialized resources used when capabilities are better provided outside the internal Darkmode structure.

Organizational Principle

Role ≠ Person. One person may perform multiple roles today, allowing responsibilities to transfer later without redesigning the operating system.

01E Roles & Responsibilities

Every meaningful responsibility within Darkmode should ultimately have an owner. Roles are defined independently of the individual performing them.
